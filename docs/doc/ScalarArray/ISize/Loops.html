    Loops with several variables on ISizeArray #

    The loops in continuation-passing style of ISizeArray.InPlace.Tail with 2 to 8 variables: Tail.forIn2, … Tail.forIn8, and Tail.forInRev2, … Tail.forInRev8 downward. Like Tail.forIn, each variable is an argument of the loop, so scalars stay unboxed, and nested loops allocate nothing. They wrap the loops of ScalarArray.Loop, and they are specified by their one-step equations, Tail.forIn2From_eq, …
