    Slices of ISizeArray #

    Slices of ISizeArray: the type, reads, slice notation, iterators, folds and searches, membership and equality, and collecting iterators into arrays.

      abbrev ScalarArray.ISizeArray.Slice :
      Type

      A region of a ISizeArray: the elements from index start up to, but not including, stop. Taking a slice doesn't copy the array. Slices are written with core's slice notation, such as a[i...j], a[i...*] or a[*...j].
